When purchasing security equipment, it is important to ensure you are dealing with officially authorized distributors.

This is especially relevant for Hikvision products in Azerbaijan.

## Risks of Unauthorized Sellers

Unauthorized sellers may lead to:

- Invalid warranty coverage
- Fake or unofficial products
- Lack of technical support

For the full official distributor statement, refer to [Hikvision's official page for Azerbaijan](https://www.hikvision.com/cis/policies/hikvision-azerbaijan-official-distributor-statement/).

## How to Verify Authorization

Customers are advised to verify distributors through [Hikvision's official global website](https://www.hikvision.com).

To ensure product authenticity and service quality, Hikvision recommends purchasing only through authorized channels.
